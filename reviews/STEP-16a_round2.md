## AMC OS Review Agent — advisory review

**PR:** local branch `wp4-step16a-ddl-foundation-platform-identity-master` (review round 2) · **Step / WP:** GOV-006 Step 16, part a (WP4) · **Reviewed at:** abb6773af44f31e00c0525ca1d4e4860536066fd

**Verdict (advisory):** BLOCK
The rewritten `platform.tg_bt_supersede` now refills every part of an old period that the new row does not cover, so no version can ever be ended. A role grant, a role's permission or a grant's scope therefore cannot be revoked from a date. That is a security weakness. Round 1 finding 6 (configuration race) is also still reproducible.
