**Verdict (advisory):** RECOMMEND CHANGES
Round 1 is largely fixed: 23 of 26 findings are closed, 2 are partly fixed (1 and 12, plus small leftovers of 23), and 1 is deliberately left open (26). One new Major remains. The new BT key rule (§3.1 rule 2) claims that nothing points at a `valid_period`-keyed table by foreign key. That is false for `accounting.account`, and following the rule drops DDL-000's declared journal-line → account foreign key.
