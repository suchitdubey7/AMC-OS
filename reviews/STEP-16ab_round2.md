**Verdict (advisory):** RECOMMEND CHANGES
The round-1 findings are fixed or partly fixed, and all tests pass. Two Majors remain. An approved role-grant request can still be edited after approval. A request can also be applied only in part, or for a shorter period than intended. Under the [ASSUMPTION] in Q87, either one can still widen a grant's scope beyond what was approved.
