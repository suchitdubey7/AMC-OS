## AMC OS Review Agent — advisory review

**PR:** branch `wp3-step11-service-catalogue` (base `wp3-step10-system-context`) · **Step / WP:** GOV-006 Step 11 / WP3 · **Reviewed at:** 63d063ffd1802317ed2fef2a82f7a475abd4a0b8

**Verdict (advisory):** RECOMMEND CHANGES
The tier table is acyclic and matches every "Calls" row. Three Major problems remain: the outbox relay design breaks the "no cross-domain writes" rule, Reporting is placed in a tier where it cannot reach the Workflow module that approvals must use, and the table moves between schemas have no ADR, which GOV-002 §2 requires.
