## AMC OS Review Agent — advisory review

**Verdict (advisory):** RECOMMEND CHANGES
The documentation checks pass and the counts are correct, but there are 7 Major findings. Several modelling choices cannot work as written: bi-temporal keys, investor-feed de-duplication, and idempotency on partitioned inbox and outbox tables. The model also conflicts with owner decision D-33, relies on an SoD premise that nothing enforces, and does not match the Draft event catalogue.
